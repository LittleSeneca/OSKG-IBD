# OSKG-IBD

> *"The microbiome is not a collection of passengers — it is an organ."*
> *— Pimentel & Rezaie, *The Microbiome Connection*

This is the knowledge graph for Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD) and Small Intestinal Bacterial Overgrowth (SIBO). Every claim, edge, and synthesis here is drawn from structured extraction of the canon: gastroenterology textbooks, SIBO-specific clinical guides, microbiome science, clinical practice guidelines, and dietary intervention research.

**Status:** Phase 5 complete. Culminating capstone published. [[notes/synthesis/Phase-5-Capstone|Read the capstone]] for the evidence-grounded synthesis of the IBD-SIBO relationship.

## Structure

- **[[Book Guide]]** — tiered reading list, acquisition status, reading order
- **[[Paper Guide]]** — research papers, reviews, meta-analyses
- **[[METHODOLOGY]]** — full pipeline, domain adaptation, fair use analysis
- **[[notes/Notes Index]]** — entry point for all knowledge domains

## Approach

- Sources are textbooks (libgen), clinical practice guidelines, and peer-reviewed research papers
- Pipeline: acquisition → reading notes → claims → typed edges → synthesis → capstone
- Every claim is traceable to its source. The graph does not summarize — it decomposes.
- Tagged and cross-referenced for evidence synthesis, not just human browsing
