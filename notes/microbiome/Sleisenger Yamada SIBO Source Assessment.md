# Sleisenger & Yamada SIBO Source Assessment

Systematic investigation of both Tier 1 gastroenterology textbooks for SIBO-relevant content beyond what was already extracted. This note documents the findings and explains why no additional reading notes were produced.

---

## Sleisenger & Fordtran: Qayed/Shahnavaz Handbook (2020)

**File:** `sources/books/Sleisenger-Fordtran-Handbook-Qayed-2020.txt` (50 MB)

**Finding:** This is a Q&A-format review companion, NOT the main Sleisenger & Fordtran gastrointestinal and liver disease textbook. (The main Sleisenger textbook could not be acquired after 8+ CDN failures across 3 QG0 re-acquisition attempts.)

**SIBO content:** Minimal. A full-text search for SIBO, bacterial overgrowth, breath test, and methane yielded only scattered mentions embedded in clinical vignette questions and answer explanations. There is no dedicated SIBO chapter. The references are incidental -- SIBO appears as a differential diagnosis option in multiple-choice questions, not as a systematic treatment of the topic.

**Examples of SIBO mentions (all in Q&A vignettes):**
- Line 18085: "(SIBO)" listed as a side-effect consideration for medications
- Line 18169: "empirically for SIBO in the past with no relief" in a patient history vignette
- Line 18229: "B. SIBO" as a multiple choice answer option
- Line 2301: "small intestinal bacterial overgrowth" in a clinical management question
- Line 3100: "breath testing for bacterial overgrowth" in an answer explanation

**Assessment:** The Qayed/Shahnavaz handbook is a board-review-style Q&A book, not a textbook chapter collection. Its value for the OSKG graph is nil for SIBO -- the mentions lack claims, evidence, citations, or argumentative structure. No reading note is warranted.

**Review Companion (2020):** Also checked. Similarly minimal: 1 match each for SIBO, bacterial overgrowth, breath test, and methane. Also Q&A format. Also does not warrant a reading note.

## Yamada's Textbook of Gastroenterology, 7E (2022)

**File:** `sources/books/Yamada-Textbook-Gastroenterology-7E-2022.txt` (156 MB)

**Chapter 58 (Bacterial Overgrowth):** Already extracted and thoroughly noted. See [[Yamada Ch58 - Bacterial Overgrowth Textbook]] (157 lines, complete with divergences from Pimentel, assessment table, and structural tension map). This is the definitive textbook SIBO reference.

**Beyond Ch58 -- SIBO Mentions in Other Chapters:**

A full-text search across all 156 MB found 251 SIBO mentions, 156 bacterial overgrowth mentions, 137+ breath test mentions, and 25+ methane mentions. These were mapped to their host chapters:

| Chapter | Approx Line Range | SIBO/BO Content | Assessment |
|---------|-------------------|-----------------|------------|

Covers SIBO as cause of steatorrhea, B12 deficiency, and protein-losing enteropathy. | SIBO content is a short subsection within a broader nutrition chapter. The content is factual (SIBO causes malabsorption) and adds nothing to the Ch58 analysis. Not worth a standalone note. |
| Ch34 (Gas and Bloating) | ~41800-42800 | Breath test methodology section with SIBO testing protocols (Table 34.9), precautions (Table 34.10), methane analysis rationale, and a clinical algorithm (Figure 34.12) that includes SIBO as a diagnostic consideration. | The most substantive SIBO content outside Ch58. However, it is entirely methodological -- how to perform breath tests, not what the results mean. | Elderly GI chapter | ~35300-35500 | SIBO mentioned as a cause of malabsorption in the elderly. Notes the ambiguity of positive lactulose breath tests (cross-references Ch58). | Brief mention. Cross-references Ch58 directly. No independent claims. |

| Ch144 (Infectious Enteritis) | ~42900, 43400 | Cross-references to SIBO chapters. | Cross-references only. |

**Key finding:** Every SIBO mention in chapters beyond Ch58 either (a) cross-references Ch58 directly ("see Chapter 58, Bacterial overgrowth"), (b) is a brief contextual mention of SIBO as one of many causes of a condition, or (c) is breath test methodology that describes how to test but makes no independent claims about SIBO as a disease entity.

**The breath test content in Ch34 merits specific comment.** The chapter includes breath test protocols (Table 34.9), interpretation precautions (Table 34.10), and a clinical algorithm that positions SIBO breath testing within the bloating workup. This is clinically useful but analytically redundant with Ch58. The Ch58 note already covers:
- Lactulose vs glucose breath test accuracy debate
- The double-peak criterion controversy
- The Posserud 2007 data (4% SIBO by aspirate, 35% by LBT)
- Prevalence inflation from breath-test-based studies
- Rapid transit confound

Ch34's methodology content does not change or add to any of these analytical claims. It provides the "how" where Ch58 provides the "what it means."

## Disposition

**No additional reading notes produced.** The two sources were systematically investigated:

1. **Sleisenger & Fordtran Handbook (Qayed/Shahnavaz):** Q&A review companion without dedicated SIBO chapter. Scattered mentions in clinical vignettes are incidental and non-claim-bearing. The main Sleisenger textbook (which likely contains a SIBO chapter) could not be acquired.

2. **Yamada 7E:** Ch58 (Bacterial Overgrowth) is already extracted and noted. Other chapters contain SIBO mentions that are either cross-references, brief contextual asides, or methodology content that is analytically redundant with Ch58. None rise to the level of independent SIBO claims requiring separate reading notes.

If the main Sleisenger & Fordtran textbook (not the Qayed handbook) is acquired later, its SIBO chapter would merit extraction as the other half of the canonical gastroenterology textbook pair alongside Yamada Ch58. The absence is a gap in the graph's coverage of mainstream GI positions on SIBO.
