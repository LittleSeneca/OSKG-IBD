## Key Events

- 1932: Crohn, Ginzburg, and Oppenheimer describe regional ileitis
- 1950s-60s: UC and Crohn's distinguished as separate entities
- 1998: Infliximab (Remicade) — first biologic approved for Crohn's
- 2000: NOD2 identified as first IBD susceptibility gene
- 2004-2006: Pimentel's early SIBO work — breath testing, rifaximin trials
- 2017: North American Breath Testing Consensus
- 2020: ACG/AGA SIBO guidelines published
- 2020: IMO (Intestinal Methanogen Overgrowth) terminology introduced
