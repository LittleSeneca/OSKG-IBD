## Dietary Protocol Summary

| Diet | Source | Mechanism | Restrictiveness | Evidence Base |
|------|--------|-----------|----------------|---------------|
| SCD | Gottschall 1994 | Monosaccharide provision; starve microbes | High (no grains, sugar, most dairy) | Anecdotal/historical |
| Low-residue / Anti-inflammatory | Foote 2020 | Reduce mechanical irritation; anti-inflammatory foods | Low-Moderate (trigger-based) | Dietetics consensus |
| Elemental Diet (EEN) | Thompson 2013 | Bowel rest + bacterial starvation + mucosal healing | Extreme (liquid only, 2-12 wks) | Strong (pediatric RCTs) |
| SIBO Specific Food Guide | Sarna/Siebecker 2021 | SCD + low-FODMAP hybrid; color-coded tiers | Moderate-High (progressive) | Clinical experience |
| Low-Fermentation Eating (LFE) | Pimentel/Rezaie 2022 | Restrict non-digestible carbs + 4-5hr meal spacing | Moderate (2 simple rules) | Clinical experience (Cedars-Sinai) |

## Key Questions

- What is the comparative efficacy of EEN vs corticosteroids for inducing remission in pediatric Crohn's?
- How does the low-FODMAP diet impact SIBO breath test results independent of symptom improvement?
- What is the mechanistic basis for the Specific Carbohydrate Diet -- is it truly about carbohydrate restriction, or is it de facto a low-FODMAP diet?
- Do all SIBO/IBD dietary protocols work through the same mechanism (fermentable carbohydrate restriction) despite different food lists?
- Is dietary therapy for SIBO/IBD curative (Gottschall) or supportive (Pimentel)?
