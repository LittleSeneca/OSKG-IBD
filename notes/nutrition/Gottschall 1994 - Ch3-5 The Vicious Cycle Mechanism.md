# Gottschall -- Ch3-5: The Vicious Cycle: Intestinal Microbes, Fermentation, and Carbohydrate Digestion

These three chapters present the complete pathophysiological model underlying the Specific Carbohydrate Diet. The argument proceeds in three stages: (Ch3) intestinal microbial ecology is disrupted in disease, creating overgrowth; (Ch4) undigested carbohydrates fuel microbial fermentation, producing acids and gas that injure the intestine, triggering a self-perpetuating cycle; (Ch5) the biochemistry of carbohydrate digestion explains why certain carbohydrates escape digestion in the damaged intestine.

**Strongest section:** Ch5's biochemistry of carbohydrate digestion -- clear, accurate, and still relevant. The microvilli diagrams and disaccharidase explanation are excellent patient education.

**Weakest section:** The SCD-elemental diet equivalence claim (Ch5) -- overreaches the evidence and is contradicted by polymeric formula trials.

**Cross-reference note:** The vicious cycle model is the SCD's signature intellectual contribution. Pimentel's SIBO model (The Microbiome Connection, 2022) refines it with specific mechanisms (anti-vinculin antibodies, MMC impairment, hydrogen vs. methane vs. hydrogen sulfide SIBO subtypes) but preserves the core structure: small intestinal bacterial overgrowth → fermentation → symptoms → perpetuation. Gottschall's 1994 model was an impressive precursor.
