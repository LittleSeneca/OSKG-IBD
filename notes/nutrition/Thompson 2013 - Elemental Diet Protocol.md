# Thompson -- The IBD Remission Diet: Elemental Diet Protocol

Jini Patel Thompson is a Crohn's patient who achieved remission through elemental diet therapy and formulated a commercial elemental shake product (Absorb Plus). The IBD Remission Diet (2nd edition, 2013) presents a comprehensive protocol combining exclusive elemental nutrition, antimicrobial herbal therapy (Wild Oregano Oil), high-dose probiotics, and a systematic four-phase food reintroduction protocol. Unlike Gottschall (SCD) which is a long-term dietary pattern, Thompson's protocol is an intensive 2-12 week medicalized intervention designed to induce remission, followed by structured food reintroduction.

## Protocol Summary

### The IBD Remission Diet Protocol

1. **Elemental Phase (2-12 weeks):** Exclusive Absorb Plus shakes (or equivalent semi-elemental formula) providing 100% of nutrition. Calories: 2000-4400/day depending on weight goals. Shakes consumed every 1.5-2 hours.

2. **Supplementation (concurrent):** Jini's Wild Oregano Oil Protocol (antimicrobial), high-dose probiotics (450+ billion CFU/day recommended), L-glutamine, vitamins/minerals. All supplements emptied into shakes.

3. **Food Reintroduction (variable duration):** Four-phase structured reintroduction. Phase 1: easiest-to-digest foods (cooked vegetables, broths, well-cooked fruits). Continue shakes as calorie base, gradually reducing as solid food increases. Test 1 food per 1-3 days.

4. **Maintenance:** Continued probiotic supplementation, identified safe foods as dietary foundation, gradual expansion of food variety as health improves.

## Cross-Cutting Assessment

| Claim | Confidence | Most Vulnerable To |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| EEN = steroid efficacy + superior mucosal healing | HIGH | Pediatric data; adult compliance limits applicability |
| Semi-elemental = elemental efficacy | MEDIUM-HIGH | Polymeric formula trials support broader claim |
| Three-mechanism model (rest/starve/heal) | MEDIUM | Mechanism unknown; microbiome modulation may be key |
| Duration guidelines | LOW | Arbitrary; no duration trials |
| Rest requirement | LOW | Untested; creates access barrier |
| Four-phase reintroduction | MEDIUM (approach) / LOW (specific phases) | No phase validation |
| Coca Pulse Test | VERY LOW | Pseudoscientific; no modern validation |

**Strongest section:** The clinical trial evidence for EEN efficacy in Ch1 -- this is real, robust data that validates the entire approach.

**Weakest section:** The Coca Pulse Test -- inclusion of pseudoscientific allergy testing damages credibility of an otherwise evidence-aware book.

**Cross-reference note:** Thompson's elemental diet represents the most intensive dietary intervention in the IBD spectrum -- more restrictive than SCD, more medicalized than any other dietary approach, and with the strongest clinical trial evidence. The irony is that the protocol with the strongest evidence is also the least accessible (cost, compliance, social disruption). The gap between what works in trials (EEN) and what's sustainable (SCD, anti-inflammatory eating) is the central tension in IBD dietary therapy.
