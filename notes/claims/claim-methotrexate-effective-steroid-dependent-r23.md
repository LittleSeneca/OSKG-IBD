# acg-cd2018-14: Methotrexate is effective for steroid-dependent CD (R23)

**Source:** [[ACG Crohn's 2018 - Lichtenstein]] — Lichtenstein et al. — ACG Clinical Guideline: Management of Crohn's Disease (2018)

## The Claim

Methotrexate is effective for steroid-dependent CD (R23)

## Evidence

Parenteral methotrexate 25 mg/week is effective for induction of remission in steroid-dependent CD and for maintenance. After 4 months of steroid-free remission on 25 mg/week, the dose may be lowered to 15 mg/week. Oral methotrexate data for maintenance are lacking. Low-dose oral methotrexate (12.5 mg/week) is effective for preventing immunogenicity to biologic agents. Contraindicated in pregnancy (teratogenic); men should avoid conception during use and for 3 months after stopping.

**Cross-guideline comparison:**
G6 (AGA 2021) similarly supports MTX as a second-line immunomodulator. G8 (ECCO 2020) discusses MTX more extensively, including its role as combination therapy with anti-TNF agents. All guidelines agree on the pregnancy contraindication.

## Assessment

MTX is underutilized relative to thiopurines, partly due to the injection requirement and pregnancy concerns, but partly due to less familiarity among gastroenterologists. The conditional recommendation (vs. strong for thiopurines) reflects the smaller evidence base. The dose reduction from 25 mg to 15 mg after 4 months is a practical pearl not widely known.
