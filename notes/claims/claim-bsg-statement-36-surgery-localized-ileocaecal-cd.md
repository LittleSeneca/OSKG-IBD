# bsg-ibd2019-6: Statement 36 (weak, low): Laparoscopic resection should be considered as an alternative to medical therapy for localized ileocaecal Crohn's disease. T

**Source:** [[BSG IBD 2019 - Lamb]] — Lamb et al. — BSG Consensus Guidelines on IBD (2019), Gut 68(Suppl 3):s1-s106

## The Claim

Statement 36 (weak, low): Laparoscopic resection should be considered as an alternative to medical therapy for localized ileocaecal Crohn's disease. The BSG guideline explicitly frames surgery as a valid primary treatment option for limited disease — not just a last resort.

## Evidence

97.3% agreement. The guideline positions surgery alongside medical therapy as a primary option, reflecting the LIR!C trial and cost-effectiveness data. This contrasts with North American practice, where surgery is typically reserved for medically refractory disease. The European tradition of earlier surgical intervention for limited disease is supported by data showing excellent long-term outcomes and reduced cumulative immunosuppression exposure.

## Confidence

**Rating:** medium
**Rationale:** See source note for full evidence evaluation.

## Stakes

Challenges the 'biologics-first' paradigm. For a patient with a 5cm terminal ileal stricture, surgery may provide years of drug-free remission vs. a lifetime of biologic therapy. However, the risk of postoperative recurrence (endoscopic in 70-90% at 1 year without prophylaxis) must be weighed.

## Disagreement

**Who disagrees:** North American guidelines (ACG 2018, AGA 2021) position surgery as a last resort after medical failure. The BSG's surgery-positive stance reflects European surgical culture and cost-effectiveness data rather than evidence of superiority.
