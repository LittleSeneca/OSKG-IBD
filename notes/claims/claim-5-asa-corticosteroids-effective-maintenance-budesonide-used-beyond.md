# acg-cd2018-22: 5-ASA and corticosteroids are NOT effective for maintenance; budesonide should not be used beyond 4 months (R46-R48)

**Source:** [[ACG Crohn's 2018 - Lichtenstein]] — Lichtenstein et al. — ACG Clinical Guideline: Management of Crohn's Disease (2018)

## The Claim

5-ASA and corticosteroids are NOT effective for maintenance; budesonide should not be used beyond 4 months (R46-R48)

## Evidence

Mesalamine: 11 placebo-controlled trials with mixed results — 4 showed benefit, 7 did not. Five meta-analyses: therapeutic advantage <10%, NNT >15. The totality of evidence supports the negative recommendation. Corticosteroids: Meta-analysis of 403 patients showed no difference from placebo at 6, 12, and 24 months, with significantly more adverse events. Budesonide: Six RCTs — 12-month relapse rates of 40-74%, not significantly different from placebo. One study showed reduced relapse at 3 months (early benefit only). Four meta-analyses show mixed results with no consistent maintenance benefit.

## Assessment

These three negative recommendations complete the therapeutic housecleaning: 5-ASA doesn't work for induction OR maintenance in CD; steroids don't work for maintenance; budesonide is only for induction. The strong GRADE ratings ("moderate" quality of evidence) make these some of the most definitive statements in CD therapeutics. Clinicians should feel confident stopping these agents in patients where they've been inappropriately continued.
