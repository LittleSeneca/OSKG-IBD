# thompson-een-5: Rest is essential during the elemental diet -- patients should take time off work and minimize activity

**Source:** [[Thompson 2013 - Elemental Diet Protocol]] — Thompson — The IBD Remission Diet (2013)

## The Claim

You really need to take time off work/school during this period and treat the process like a healing vacation... The IBD Remission Diet is an intensive healing program and it takes a lot of energy for your body to heal itself... you have to rest and conserve your energy for healing work -- not other work.

## Evidence

Anecdotal: a patient who worked full-time while on the diet showed minimal improvement. Thompson attributes this to energy diversion from healing to work.

## Stakes

## Assessment

Thompson's rest recommendation reflects maximalist healing philosophy, not evidence. It may help some patients and exclude others. A more evidence-based approach would be to recommend reduced activity without requiring complete work cessation. The horizontal positioning advice for bleeding is potentially dangerous if it delays seeking emergency care for hemorrhage.
