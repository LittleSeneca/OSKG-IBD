# chutkan-found-2: The Western microbiome is depleted, with Americans harboring significantly fewer bacterial species than traditional societies

**Source:** [[The Microbiome Solution - Chutkan - Intro Ch1-3]] — Chutkan — The Microbiome Solution (2015), Intro + Ch 1-3

## The Claim

Modern Westerners have lost roughly a third of their microbial diversity compared to hunter-gatherer and traditional agricultural populations. This loss is driven by processed foods, antibiotics, C-sections, formula feeding, and hyper-sanitized environments.

## Evidence

References studies comparing Hadza hunter-gatherers, rural African populations, and Amerindians in the Venezuelan Amazon to Western populations. Notes that traditional populations consuming high-fiber diets have microbial species not found in any Western gut.
