# aga-uc2020-4: In infliximab-exposed patients (especially primary non-responders), ustekinumab or tofacitinib are preferred over vedolizumab or adalimumab

**Source:** [[AGA UC 2020 - Feuerstein]] — Feuerstein et al. — AGA Clinical Practice Guidelines: Ulcerative Colitis (2020)

## The Claim

In infliximab-exposed patients (especially primary non-responders), ustekinumab or tofacitinib are preferred over vedolizumab or adalimumab

## Evidence

Separate NMA of 7 RCTs in 1580 patients with prior TNF exposure (infliximab and golimumab excluded — they only studied biologic-naïve). Tofacitinib vs. adalimumab: OR 11.05 (1.79-68.41). Ustekinumab vs. adalimumab: OR 10.71 (2.01-57.20). Tofacitinib vs. vedolizumab: OR 6.18 (1.00-38.00). Ustekinumab vs. vedolizumab: OR 5.99 (1.13-31.76). All comparisons rated down for intransitivity (prior treatment exposure is an effect modifier; study-level data didn't report proportion with >1 TNF exposure or multi-class exposure). VARSITY subset (~21% with prior non-adalimumab TNF exposure): no significant difference vedolizumab vs. adalimumab at week 52 (20.3% vs. 16.0%). The guideline explicitly limits these recommendations to infliximab-exposed patients — benefit of switching in adalimumab- or golimumab-exposed patients is uncertain.

## Assessment

The OR magnitude for tofacitinib and ustekinumab over adalimumab (OR ~11) is implausibly large and almost certainly reflects confounding by indication in the underlying trials (sicker patients, more refractory disease, differential prior exposure). The wide CIs make these estimates nearly uninterpretable. The practical message — ustekinumab or tofacitinib over a second anti-TNF after primary infliximab failure — aligns with the ACG's out-of-class switching principle, but the ranking of ustekinumab/tofacitinib over vedolizumab is on very thin ice. The VARSITY subgroup analysis showing no difference between vedolizumab and adalimumab in TNF-exposed patients directly contradicts the NMA's OR of 5.99 for ustekinumab vs. vedolizumab. The evidence doesn't support ranking vedolizumab below ustekinumab/tofacitinib in this population; the VARSITY data suggest equipoise.
