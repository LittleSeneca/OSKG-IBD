# chutkan-rewild-1: Patients should actively question antibiotic prescriptions using a structured set of questions — physician prescribing behavior is influenced by perceived patient expectations

**Source:** [[The Microbiome Solution - Chutkan - Ch11 Rewilding Illness]] — Chutkan — The Microbiome Solution (2015), Ch 11

## The Claim

Doctors prescribe more antibiotics when they perceive the patient wants one. A structured set of 10 questions (Table 11-1) can signal that the patient is "not keen on taking an antibiotic unless absolutely necessary" and shift the clinical conversation from automatic prescribing to shared decision-making.

## Evidence

Cites studies showing that "doctors are far more likely to prescribe an antibiotic when they perceive that the patient is interested in taking one." The ten questions range from the foundational ("Is the antibiotic absolutely necessary?") to the practical ("What's the shortest amount of time I can take it for?") to the risk-assessment ("What's the worst thing that could happen if I don't take an antibiotic?").
