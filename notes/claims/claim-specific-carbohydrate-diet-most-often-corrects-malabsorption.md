# gottschall-ch35-8: The Specific Carbohydrate Diet is biochemically equivalent to the Elemental Diet in providing monosaccharides that require no digestion

**Source:** [[Gottschall 1994 - Ch3-5 The Vicious Cycle Mechanism]] — Gottschall — Breaking the Vicious Cycle (1994), Ch 3-5

## The Claim

The Specific Carbohydrate Diet most often corrects malabsorption allowing nutrients to enter the bloodstream and be made available to the cells of the body thereby strengthening the immune system's ability to fight.

## Evidence

Reiteration of the monosaccharide principle from Ch1. The SCD allows glucose, fructose, and galactose (monosaccharides found in honey, fruit, properly fermented yoghurt). It bans lactose, sucrose, maltose, isomaltose (disaccharides) and most starches (polysaccharides). Exception: legume starches (dried beans, lentils, split peas) are permitted after soaking because "they have been shown to be tolerated." The legumes must be soaked 10-12 hours to remove indigestible sugars.

**Rating:** low
**Rationale:** LOW for the claim that SCD = elemental diet in effect. The SCD is not biochemically equivalent to an elemental diet. Elemental diets provide amino acids (not whole proteins), glucose (not fruit/honey 

## Stakes

If SCD truly replicates elemental diet effects through carbohydrate restriction alone, it offers a sustainable oral alternative to tube-fed EEN. This would be enormously significant for IBD treatment.

## Disagreement

**Who disagrees:** Pediatric IBD specialists use EEN because it provides complete bowel rest (no whole proteins, no complex substrates of any kind), not just because it provides monosaccharides. The polymeric vs. elemental formula trials (comparing whole-protein to amino-acid formulas) show similar efficacy, suggesting the mechanism is bowel rest or microbiome modulation, not monosaccharide provision.

**Alternative reading:** SCD may be effective through a different mechanism than EEN -- not monosaccharide provision, but reduction of fermentable substrate and elimination of common dietary triggers (gluten, dairy proteins, emulsifiers).

## Assessment

This is Gottschall's most ambitious claim and her weakest. The SCD-EEN equivalence is asserted but never tested. The fact that polymeric formulas (with complex carbohydrates and whole proteins) induce remission as effectively as elemental formulas undermines the monosaccharide hypothesis. SCD may work, but probably not for the reason Gottschall proposes.

**Strongest section:** Ch5's biochemistry of carbohydrate digestion -- clear, accurate, and still relevant. The microvilli diagrams and disaccharidase explanation are excellent patient education.

**Weakest section:** The SCD-elemental diet equivalence claim (Ch5) -- overreaches the evidence and is contradicted by polymeric formula trials.

**Cross-reference note:** The vicious cycle model is the SCD's signature intellectual contribution. Pimentel's SIBO model (The Microbiome Connection, 2022) refines it with specific mechanisms (anti-vinculin antibodies, MMC impairment, hydrogen vs. methane vs. hydrogen sulfide SIBO subtypes) but preserves the core structure: small intestinal bacterial overgrowth → fermentation → symptoms → perpetuation. Gottschall's 1994 model was an impressive precursor.
