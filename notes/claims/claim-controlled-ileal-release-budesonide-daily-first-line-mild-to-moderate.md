# acg-cd2018-9: Controlled ileal release budesonide 9 mg daily is first-line for mild-to-moderate ileocecal CD (R12)

**Source:** [[ACG Crohn's 2018 - Lichtenstein]] — Lichtenstein et al. — ACG Clinical Guideline: Management of Crohn's Disease (2018)

## The Claim

Controlled ileal release budesonide 9 mg daily is first-line for mild-to-moderate ileocecal CD (R12)

## Evidence

CIR budesonide is a pH-dependent ileal-release oral corticosteroid with high topical activity and low systemic bioavailability (~10-20%). Demonstrated effective in RCTs for active mild-to-moderate ileocecal CD (Greenberg 1994, Tremaine 2002). The lesser efficacy compared with conventional corticosteroids is balanced by the favorable safety profile (limited systemic exposure). The anatomic limitation is important: effective only for terminal ileum and right colon disease.

## Assessment

Budesonide fills a genuine clinical niche — patients with mild ileocecal disease who need more than symptomatic therapy but don't yet warrant systemic steroids or biologics. The "low" evidence quality reflects the age and size of available trials, not the consistency of the effect. The delivery system limitation is a key practical point: budesonide won't work for jejunal, proximal ileal, or left-sided colonic disease.
