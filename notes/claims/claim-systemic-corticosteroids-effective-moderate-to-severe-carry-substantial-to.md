# ecco-cd2020-3: Systemic corticosteroids are effective for moderate-to-severe CD but carry substantial toxicity; they are a bridge, not a destination (R1.3)

**Source:** [[ECCO Crohn's Medical 2020 - Torres]] — Torres et al. — ECCO Guidelines on Crohn's Disease: Medical Management (2020)

## The Claim

Systemic corticosteroids are effective for moderate-to-severe CD but carry substantial toxicity; they are a bridge, not a destination (R1.3)

## Evidence

Pooled data from two RCTs (n=267): corticosteroids were twice as effective as placebo for clinical remission (RR 1.99; 95% CI 1.51-2.64). Clinical response with methylprednisolone was 93.6% vs. 53.4% for placebo (RR 1.75). However, adverse events were 5-fold higher (31.8% vs. 6.5%; RR 4.89). Steroid-related AEs include Cushing syndrome, acne, infection (increased abscess risk), ecchymoses, hypertension, diabetes, osteoporosis, cataracts, glaucoma. The guideline defines **corticosteroid dependency/excess** as: inability to wean below prednisolone 10 mg/day (or budesonide 3 mg/day) within 3 months, relapse within 3 months of stopping, or need for >1 course in 12 months — any of these should trigger a steroid-sparing strategy. Conventional steroids do not prevent disease relapse and are ineffective for maintenance.

**Cross-guideline comparison:**
G1 (ACG 2018, R17-R18) gives a **strong** recommendation for short-term steroid use (vs. ECCO's "weak") — this is a notable divergence. ACG's separate R18 (conditional) explicitly states steroids don't achieve mucosal healing. ECCO folds both messages into one weaker recommendation. The steroid-dependency definition is more precisely defined in G8 than G1.

## Assessment

The "weak" recommendation for systemic steroids (vs. ACG's "strong") is puzzling — both agree steroids work, but ECCO weights the toxicity more heavily. This may reflect European reluctance to endorse a toxic therapy strongly, even when effective. The steroid-dependency definition is clinically invaluable: it provides a concrete trigger for treatment escalation. The 3-month wean benchmark should be tracked in every CD patient on steroids.
