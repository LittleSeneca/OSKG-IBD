# acg-cd2018-15: Anti-TNF agents are effective for steroid-refractory and thiopurine-refractory CD; combination therapy with infliximab + azathioprine is superior to either alone (R24-R26)

**Source:** [[ACG Crohn's 2018 - Lichtenstein]] — Lichtenstein et al. — ACG Clinical Guideline: Management of Crohn's Disease (2018)

## The Claim

Anti-TNF agents are effective for steroid-refractory and thiopurine-refractory CD; combination therapy with infliximab + azathioprine is superior to either alone (R24-R26)

## Evidence

R26 is one of only five recommendations with "high" quality evidence, based on the landmark SONIC trial (Colombel 2010). Anti-TNF agents are rapid in onset (benefit often within 2 weeks), effective for induction of response, remission, and mucosal healing. They appear more effective when given earlier in the disease course (within 2 years of onset). Pre-treatment screening required: latent TB (tuberculin skin test ± IGRA), hepatitis B (HBsAg, HBsAb, HBcAb), and assessment for other latent infections (histoplasmosis, blastomycosis). Vaccination status should be reviewed and updated before initiation.

**Biosimilars (Summary Statements 43-44):** Biosimilar infliximab and adalimumab are effective for *de novo* induction and maintenance. Insufficient data exist to support switching between biosimilars in stable patients. A large phase 4 trial (NOR-SWITCH) showed non-inferiority of switching from originator to CT-P13.

**Cross-guideline comparison:**
G6 (AGA 2021) now recommends early biologic therapy in moderate-severe CD, which is a stronger position than this 2018 ACG guideline. G8 (ECCO 2020) provides detailed algorithms for biologic selection and sequencing. The SONIC trial data (R26) is the single most important evidence in CD therapeutics and is cited by all subsequent guidelines.

## Assessment

R26 (combination therapy) is the crown jewel of the guideline — "high" quality evidence, strong recommendation. The SONIC trial showed that infliximab + azathioprine achieved steroid-free remission in 57% vs. 44% for infliximab alone and 30% for azathioprine alone at week 26. However, the guideline is cautious about *continuing* an immunomodulator when anti-TNF is added after immunomodulator failure — direct evidence is lacking. This is a major uncertainty that later guidelines attempt to address with TDM-based approaches.
