# pimentel-ch9-3: SIBO is present in >50% of IBD patients with persistent symptoms despite controlled inflammation

**Source:** [[Pimentel 2022 - Ch9 Refractory SIBO]] — Pimentel & Rezaie — The Microbiome Connection (2022), Ch 9

## The Claim

We have shown that more than 50 percent of such patients [IBD with controlled inflammation but persistent symptoms] have SIBO.

## Evidence

Pimentel's published data on SIBO in quiescent IBD. Clinical observation. The mechanism: IBD inflammation damages gut motility and creates strictures, both of which promote SIBO.

## Confidence

**Rating:** high
**Rationale:** MEDIUM-HIGH. This is published data from Pimentel's group. The mechanism is plausible. If validated, this is a clinically important finding: it means many IBD patients on escalating immunosuppression 

## Stakes

This is one of the most actionable claims in the chapter. If >50% of IBD patients with persistent symptoms despite controlled inflammation have SIBO, then breath testing and SIBO treatment should be standard practice in IBD management. This could prevent unnecessary escalation of immunosuppression, biologic switching, or surgery.

## Assessment

The IBD-SIBO overlap is an underrecognized clinical problem. Pimentel's 50%+ figure needs independent validation but aligns with clinical experience (IBD patients often have bloating/gas/bloating that doesn't respond to anti-inflammatories). This is one area where Pimentel's SIBO framework could most immediately improve clinical practice.
