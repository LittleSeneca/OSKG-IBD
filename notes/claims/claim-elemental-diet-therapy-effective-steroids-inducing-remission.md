# thompson-een-1: Elemental diet therapy is as effective as corticosteroids for inducing remission in pediatric Crohn's, with superior mucosal healing

**Source:** [[Thompson 2013 - Elemental Diet Protocol]] — Thompson — The IBD Remission Diet (2013)

## The Claim

Elemental diet therapy is as effective as steroids in inducing remission, whilst avoiding steroid side effects.

## Evidence

Two pediatric trials from England and Italy. Thompson quotes Dr. Robert Canani: "In children with active Crohn's disease, exclusive nutritional therapy shows a more rapid effect than steroids in inducing clinical remission and is markedly more effective than steroids in producing healing of mucosal inflammation."

## Confidence

**Rating:** high
**Rationale:** HIGH. The efficacy of exclusive enteral nutrition (EEN) for pediatric Crohn's is one of the best-established findings in IBD dietary therapy. Multiple meta-analyses confirm EEN equivalence to corticos

## Stakes

If elemental diet is as effective as steroids with better mucosal healing and fewer side effects, it should be first-line therapy for pediatric Crohn's. The fact that it's not widely used in adults (due to palatability and compliance issues) is a delivery problem, not an efficacy problem.

## Disagreement

**Who disagrees:** No one disputes the pediatric data. The controversy is: (1) whether EEN is equally effective in adults (compliance is much worse), (2) whether polymeric formulas (whole protein) work as well as elemental (amino acid) formulas (trials suggest they do, undermining the "elemental" rationale), and (3) what the mechanism actually is (bowel rest? microbiome modulation? elimination of dietary antigens?).

**Alternative reading:** Not documented.

## Assessment

This is the strongest evidence base for ANY dietary therapy in IBD. Thompson correctly identifies EEN as a validated intervention. Her framing of Absorb Plus within this evidence is reasonable but the product itself has not been directly tested in trials -- the evidence is for EEN as a class, not for her specific formulation.
