# chutkan-found-4: The rise in autoimmune and allergic diseases is causally linked to reduced microbial exposure in early life

**Source:** [[The Microbiome Solution - Chutkan - Intro Ch1-3]] — Chutkan — The Microbiome Solution (2015), Intro + Ch 1-3

## The Claim

We have fewer bacteria in our gut than our ancestors because we live in an environment that's too clean, and our overly robust hygiene practices, along with the widespread use of antibiotics, have left us with a legacy of smaller, less diverse microbiomes that are less resistant and resilient.

## Evidence

Cites the inverse relationship between infectious disease burden and autoimmune disease prevalence. Notes that children raised on farms, with pets, and in larger families have lower rates of asthma, allergy, and autoimmune disease. Discusses the Old Friends hypothesis.
