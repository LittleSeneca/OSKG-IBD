# acg-uc2019-4: Anxiety and depression screening should be routine in UC care

**Source:** [[ACG UC 2019 - Rubin]] — Rubin et al. — ACG Clinical Guideline: Ulcerative Colitis (2019)

## The Claim

Anxiety and depression screening should be routine in UC care

## Evidence

Active UC is associated with higher rates of anxiety, depression, and impaired social/career function. The guideline cites Regueiro et al. (2017) on the bidirectional relationship between psychosocial comorbidities and IBD outcomes, but does not make a graded recommendation. This is appropriately left as a key concept since screening is a process measure, not a therapeutic intervention amenable to GRADE.

## Assessment

This key concept was ahead of its time in a GI guideline and reflects Rubin's longstanding interest in the brain-gut axis. By 2024 standards it undersells the evidence: multiple studies now show that untreated depression/anxiety independently predicts IBD flares and healthcare utilization. The AGA's 2021 guideline on mental health in IBD would later strengthen this recommendation.
