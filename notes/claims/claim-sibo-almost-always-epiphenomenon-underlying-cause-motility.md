# acg-sibo2020-6: SIBO is almost always an epiphenomenon -- the underlying cause (motility disorder, structural abnormality, medication) must be identified to prevent recurrence

**Source:** [[ACG SIBO 2020 - Pimentel]] — Pimentel et al. — ACG Clinical Guideline: Small Intestinal Bacterial Overgrowth (2020)

## The Claim

SIBO is almost always an epiphenomenon -- the underlying cause (motility disorder, structural abnormality, medication) must be identified to prevent recurrence

## Evidence

The guideline lists protective mechanisms: gastric acid barrier, bile acids, antegrade peristalsis, the migrating motor complex (MMC) -- particularly phase III, which sweeps bacteria from the small bowel toward the colon, and the ileocecal valve preventing colonic reflux. Breakdown in any of these can cause SIBO. Associated conditions (Table 4) include: motility disorders (scleroderma, diabetes, gastroparesis, chronic intestinal pseudo-obstruction), structural abnormalities (small intestinal diverticula, strictures, blind loop, fistulae, adhesions), postsurgical states (gastrectomy, ileocecal valve resection, bariatric surgery), medications (opioids, anticholinergics, PPI -- controversial), and systemic conditions (cirrhosis, chronic pancreatitis, cystic fibrosis, immunodeficiency).

**Recommendation 4:** "We suggest against the use of breath testing for the diagnosis of SIBO in asymptomatic patients on PPIs." Conditional recommendation, very low level of evidence.

## Assessment

This is the most clinically important non-treatment recommendation. The emphasis on identifying and treating the underlying cause -- rather than cycling antibiotics indefinitely -- is sound. The recommendation against testing asymptomatic PPI users is pragmatic: the association between PPIs and SIBO exists but is weak, and testing asymptomatic patients leads to overtreatment. The recurrence data (12.6% at 3 months, 27.5% at 6 months, 43.7% at 9 months post-treatment) underscores why prevention matters: nearly half of patients will have SIBO again within a year if the underlying cause is not addressed.
