# gottschall-fw-1: The SCD transcends oversimplifications common in dietary treatment of GI disorders

**Source:** [[Gottschall 1994 - Foreword]] — Gottschall — Breaking the Vicious Cycle (1994), Foreword

## The Claim

Hoffman states the SCD is "simply presented, yet sophisticated in its conception" and "transcends several oversimplifications to which patients with gastrointestinal problems and their physicians often fall prey."

## Evidence

Hoffman's clinical experience: "Patients from many parts of the country have consulted me. Many complain of symptoms consistent with irritable bowel syndrome. Others have been diagnosed formally with classic inflammatory bowel disease." Standard approaches (natural digestive aids, intestinal flora replacement, elimination diets, antifungal drugs, antibiotics) failed many patients.

## Stakes

If the SCD genuinely succeeds where elimination diets, antibiotics, and antifungals fail, its mechanism must be different from simple allergen avoidance or antimicrobial therapy. This supports the carbohydrate-fermentation model.

## Disagreement

**Who disagrees:** The foreword explicitly names the disagreement: "most gastroenterologists are, sadly, not even curious. They scarcely acknowledge the role diet can play." Hoffman cites a Lancet article demonstrating efficacy of exclusion diet in Crohn's disease that "has not prompted a single gastroenterologist in my large metropolitan community to administer a facsimile."

**Alternative reading:** Hoffman's positive results could be explained by the SCD being a de facto elimination diet that happens to remove common food triggers, rather than by its carbohydrate-specific mechanism.

## Assessment

The foreword is persuasive as clinical testimony, not as evidence. Hoffman is an advocate, not a researcher. But his observation that the SCD works where other dietary approaches fail is a useful data point for the graph. The key question he raises -- why gastroenterologists ignore dietary therapy despite evidence -- is a structural problem in the field, not unique to SCD.
