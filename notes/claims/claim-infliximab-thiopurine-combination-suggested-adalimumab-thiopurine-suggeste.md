## Assessment

R5A-R5C represent a more nuanced, less dogmatic approach to combination therapy than ACG 2018. The stratification by biologic is clinically rational: infliximab is the most immunogenic anti-TNF and benefits most from combination; adalimumab is less immunogenic and the benefit is uncertain; vedolizumab and ustekinumab have minimal immunogenicity and combination therapy is untested. However, Recommendation 6's "no recommendation" on de-escalation is frustrating for clinicians managing patients on long-term combination therapy who want to reduce immunosuppression. The Roblin 2020 data (adding thiopurine at time of switch) is a practice-changing pearl buried in the discussion — it suggests that even patients with pharmacokinetic failure may benefit from combination if switching within class.
