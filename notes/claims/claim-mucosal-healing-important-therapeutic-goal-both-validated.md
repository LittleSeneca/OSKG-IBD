# ecco-diag2019-6: Mucosal healing is an important therapeutic goal in both UC and CD; FC is a validated surrogate marker; transmural healing (assessed by MRE/IUS) is an emerging endpoint

**Source:** [[ECCO Diagnostic 2019 - Maaser]] — Maaser et al. — ECCO-ESGAR Guideline for Diagnostic Assessment in IBD (2019)

## The Claim

Mucosal healing is an important therapeutic goal in both UC and CD; FC is a validated surrogate marker; transmural healing (assessed by MRE/IUS) is an emerging endpoint

## Evidence

**Statement 2.1.1:** Response to treatment in active UC determined by combination of clinical parameters, endoscopy, and laboratory markers (CRP, FC) [EL1]. **Statement 2.1.2:** MH should be determined endoscopically or by FC ~3-6 months after treatment initiation [EL5]. **Statement 2.1.4:** Clinical/biochemical response in CD should be determined within 12 weeks [EL2]; endoscopic/transmural response within 6 months [EL5].

## Assessment

The transmural healing concept represents a European advance: CD is transmural, and mucosal-only assessment may miss residual disease. The TRUST trial provides the strongest IUS monitoring data. The 3-6 month reassessment window is practical guidance from trial protocols (EL5, reflecting absence of evidence for optimal timing).
