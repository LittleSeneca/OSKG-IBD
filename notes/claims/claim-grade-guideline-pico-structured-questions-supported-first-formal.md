# aga-cd2021-1: This is a GRADE guideline with PICO-structured questions, supported by the first formal network meta-analysis in a CD guideline

**Source:** [[AGA Crohn's 2021 - Feuerstein]] — Feuerstein et al. — AGA Clinical Practice Guidelines: Crohn's Disease (2021)

## The Claim

This is a GRADE guideline with PICO-structured questions, supported by the first formal network meta-analysis in a CD guideline

## Evidence

**Scope:** Adult outpatients with moderate to severe luminal CD (CDAI ≥220 or high-risk features per IOIBD criteria) and perianal fistulizing CD. Surgical management, TDM, and postoperative prophylaxis are excluded — the latter is a notable gap compared with ACG 2018.

## Assessment

The network meta-analysis is this guideline's most important methodological contribution. For the first time, clinicians have evidence-based (if indirect) guidance on biologic sequencing. However, the authors appropriately flag the limitations: no head-to-head trials exist; the analysis assumes similar trial populations and designs; and real-world patients (elderly, multi-class failures, comorbid) differ from trial populations. The MCID threshold of 10% is reasonable but means that drugs with 5-9% absolute benefit (certolizumab, vedolizumab for induction) fall below the clinical significance bar — a judgment call that some clinicians may dispute.
