# aga-uc2020-8: IV methylprednisolone 40-60 mg/day is sufficient; higher doses add toxicity without efficacy

**Source:** [[AGA UC 2020 - Feuerstein]] — Feuerstein et al. — AGA Clinical Practice Guidelines: Ulcerative Colitis (2020)

## The Claim

IV methylprednisolone 40-60 mg/day is sufficient; higher doses add toxicity without efficacy

## Evidence

No head-to-head trials of different corticosteroid doses. Turner et al. systematic review: mean dose across studies was 68 mg/day (range 40-100 mg); on meta-regression controlling for baseline severity, no correlation between dose and colectomy risk (R²<0.01). Different regimens (once daily vs. divided vs. continuous infusion) showed no superiority. Corticosteroid trials should last 3-5 days; continued use beyond 7 days is ineffective in non-responders.

## Assessment

The 40-60 mg/day range gives clinicians flexibility but may create confusion — 40 mg and 60 mg are different doses. The ACG's firm 60 mg/day recommendation is simpler to implement. Both guidelines agree that dose escalation beyond this range is futile, which is the clinically important point. The lack of correlation between dose and colectomy (R²<0.01) is decisive — if higher doses worked, we would have seen a signal.
