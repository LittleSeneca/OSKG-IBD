# gottschall-ch12-1: The Haas Specific Carbohydrate Diet cured hundreds of celiac cases before the gluten-free era

**Source:** [[Gottschall 1994 - Ch1-2 Origins and Scientific Evidence]] — Gottschall — Breaking the Vicious Cycle (1994), Ch 1-2

## The Claim

In 1951, after many years of clinical experience, Drs. Sidney V. and Merrill P. Haas published a book entitled Management of Celiac Disease... When patients followed this Specific Carbohydrate Diet for a minimum of one year, they were then able to return to a normal diet with complete and permanent disappearance of symptoms.

## Evidence

Gottschall's personal testimony: her 8-year-old daughter, diagnosed with "incurable ulcerative colitis" and deteriorating on cortisone and sulfonamides, was placed on the Haas diet and "within two years she was free of symptoms. She returned to eating normally after another few years, and has remained in excellent health for over twenty years." Additional anecdotal reports from Gottschall's own practice with "students, friends, and others" suffering from UC, Crohn's, celiac (not cured by gluten-free diet), diverticulitis, and chronic diarrhea.

## Stakes

If the Haas diet genuinely cured IBD in the 1950s, the failure of modern gastroenterology to investigate carbohydrate-restricted diets represents a massive missed opportunity. But if these were misdiagnosed cases or spontaneous remissions, the claim is dangerously misleading.

## Disagreement

**Who disagrees:** Modern celiac research: celiac is an autoimmune reaction to gluten proteins, not a carbohydrate malabsorption problem. The gluten-free diet is the only validated treatment. Mainstream gastroenterology would not accept SCD as a "cure" for IBD without controlled trials.

**Alternative reading:** Not documented.

## Assessment

This is testimonial, not scientific. The daughter's story is compelling but proves nothing about mechanism. The historical value is in establishing that carbohydrate-restricted diets for IBD have a clinical lineage predating modern drug therapy -- this is the SCD's claim to tradition, not to evidence.
