# acg-uc2019-9: Anti-TNF therapy (infliximab, adalimumab, golimumab) is high-quality evidence for induction; combination therapy with infliximab + thiopurine is superior to monotherapy

**Source:** [[ACG UC 2019 - Rubin]] — Rubin et al. — ACG Clinical Guideline: Ulcerative Colitis (2019)

## The Claim

Anti-TNF therapy (infliximab, adalimumab, golimumab) is high-quality evidence for induction; combination therapy with infliximab + thiopurine is superior to monotherapy

## Evidence

All three anti-TNF agents demonstrated superiority over placebo in registration trials (ACT-1/2 for infliximab, ULTRA-2 for adalimumab, PURSUIT for golimumab). Network meta-analyses show a non-significant trend toward higher remission with infliximab vs adalimumab or golimumab, but no head-to-head trials exist. The UC-SUCCESS trial: combination infliximab + azathioprine superior to either monotherapy for steroid-free remission at 16 weeks. Observational data: adalimumab associated with higher hospitalization rates vs infliximab in Danish cohort. Methotrexate combination data is extrapolated from CD; no direct UC evidence.

**Cross-guideline comparison:**
G5 (AGA UC 2020) makes the same strong recommendation for anti-TNF therapy. The combination therapy recommendation is infliximab-specific in both guidelines. G9 (ECCO 2022) also endorses combination therapy but discusses the growing role of vedolizumab and tofacitinib as alternatives, potentially before anti-TNF.

## Assessment

The "high quality of evidence" rating for anti-TNF induction (one of only two in the entire guideline) reflects the large, well-designed phase 3 programs. However, the absence of head-to-head trials means the choice among anti-TNF agents remains largely based on patient preference (subcutaneous vs IV), payer formulary, and provider familiarity. The combination therapy recommendation is strong for infliximab but the guideline's silence on whether adalimumab or golimumab should also be combined with an immunomodulator is a notable gap -- most experts extrapolate from the infliximab and CD data and recommend combination for all anti-TNF agents.
