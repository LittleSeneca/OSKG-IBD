# ecco-diag2019-2: Ileocolonoscopy with biopsies is required for diagnosis; endoscopic and histological features differ between CD and UC; the earliest UC histological feature is basal plasmacytosis

**Source:** [[ECCO Diagnostic 2019 - Maaser]] — Maaser et al. — ECCO-ESGAR Guideline for Diagnostic Assessment in IBD (2019)

## The Claim

Ileocolonoscopy with biopsies is required for diagnosis; endoscopic and histological features differ between CD and UC; the earliest UC histological feature is basal plasmacytosis

## Evidence

**Statement 1.6:** Ileocolonoscopy with biopsies from inflamed and uninflamed segments required [EL1]; sigmoidoscopy may suffice in acute severe colitis [EL3]. **Statement 1.7:** No single endoscopic feature is specific [EL2]; UC features = continuous, confluent colonic involvement with clear demarcation and rectal involvement; CD features = discontinuous lesions, strictures, fistulae, perianal involvement.

## Assessment

The EL1 rating for ileocolonoscopy with biopsies is appropriate — this is the diagnostic bedrock. The temporal histopathology description (early basal plasmacytosis → later crypt distortion) is the guideline's most clinically useful histopathology contribution, directly explaining why repeat endoscopy may be needed when initial biopsies are non-diagnostic.
