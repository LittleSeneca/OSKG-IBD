# aga-uc2020-9: Adjunctive antibiotics should not be used routinely in ASUC without infection

**Source:** [[AGA UC 2020 - Feuerstein]] — Feuerstein et al. — AGA Clinical Practice Guidelines: Ulcerative Colitis (2020)

## The Claim

Adjunctive antibiotics should not be used routinely in ASUC without infection

## Evidence

4 RCTs, heterogeneous antibiotics, 5-10 day courses. Overall RR for colectomy 0.79 (0.46-1.35). Excluding the oral vancomycin trial (which used an insensitive C. difficile test), RR 0.95 (0.55-1.64). Quality rated very low due to risk of bias, imprecision, inconsistency.
