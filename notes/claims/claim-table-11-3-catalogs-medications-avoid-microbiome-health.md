# chutkan-rewild-3: Multiple common medications beyond antibiotics should be avoided in dysbiosis patients, including PPIs, steroids, hormonal contraceptives, and NSAIDs

**Source:** [[The Microbiome Solution - Chutkan - Ch11 Rewilding Illness]] — Chutkan — The Microbiome Solution (2015), Ch 11

## The Claim

Table 11-3 catalogs the "Medications to Avoid" for microbiome health: broad-spectrum antibiotics (including topical forms), acid-suppressing drugs (particularly beyond 8 weeks), oral/IV corticosteroids, birth control pills, hormone replacement therapy, and NSAIDs including aspirin.

## Evidence

Reiterates and organizes the evidence from Ch 4-5. Specific tapering guidance for PPIs (rebound acid hypersecretion) and steroids (adrenal suppression).
