# acg-cd2018-11: For low-risk patients, symptomatic treatment with careful observation is acceptable (R16)

**Source:** [[ACG Crohn's 2018 - Lichtenstein]] — Lichtenstein et al. — ACG Clinical Guideline: Management of Crohn's Disease (2018)

## The Claim

For low-risk patients, symptomatic treatment with careful observation is acceptable (R16)

## Evidence

Some patients have genuinely mild, non-progressive disease. Dietary therapies (elemental, semi-elemental, defined diets) may reduce mucosal inflammation but benefits are not durable upon resumption of unrestricted diet. The key safeguard: providers must follow carefully for signs of disease worsening or progression, with the goal of normalizing objective indicators of mucosal inflammation.

## Assessment

This is a pragmatic acknowledgment that not every CD patient needs biologics. The "very low" evidence quality is honest — there are no RCTs of "watchful waiting" vs. early biologic therapy. The critical caveat is "careful observation" with objective monitoring. This recommendation must be read in context: only for truly low-risk patients. High-risk features (young age, extensive disease, perianal involvement, penetrating phenotype) should push toward earlier effective therapy.
