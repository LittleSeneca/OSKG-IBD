# chutkan-dysbiosis-2: Multiple drug classes beyond antibiotics cause or exacerbate dysbiosis

**Source:** [[The Microbiome Solution - Chutkan - Ch5 Dysbiosis]] — Chutkan — The Microbiome Solution (2015), Ch 5

## The Claim

Proton pump inhibitors (PPIs) and acid suppressants create a hospitable environment for bacterial overgrowth by removing the stomach's acid barrier. NSAIDs increase intestinal permeability ("make holes in your gut") allowing bacterial translocation. Birth control pills and hormone replacement therapy alter the microbial ecosystem through estrogen effects. Corticosteroids suppress beneficial bacteria and allow fungal proliferation. Chemotherapy poisons microbial cells along with cancer cells.

## Evidence

For PPIs: increased risk of C. difficile infection and pneumonia, altered gut bacterial composition, small intestinal bacterial overgrowth. For NSAIDs: well-established ulceration and permeability effects. For hormones: association with chronic yeast infections. For steroids: clinical observation of fungal overgrowth in steroid users. For chemo: known myelosuppression and mucosal damage.
