# acg-uc2019-11: Vedolizumab and tofacitinib are effective for induction, including in anti-TNF-exposed patients; both earn strong recommendations

**Source:** [[ACG UC 2019 - Rubin]] — Rubin et al. — ACG Clinical Guideline: Ulcerative Colitis (2019)

## The Claim

Vedolizumab and tofacitinib are effective for induction, including in anti-TNF-exposed patients; both earn strong recommendations

## Evidence

GEMINI 1: vedolizumab week 6 clinical remission 16.9% vs 5.4% placebo (P=0.001); mucosal healing 40.9% vs 24.8%. Post-hoc analysis confirmed efficacy in anti-TNF failures. OCTAVE 1 and 2: tofacitinib 10 mg BID week 8 remission 18.5% and 16.6% vs 8.2% and 3.6% placebo. Half of OCTAVE patients had prior anti-TNF failure. Herpes zoster: 5.1% with tofacitinib 10 mg BID vs 0.5% placebo. The guideline makes no recommendations about sequencing among these agents due to absence of head-to-head trials.

**Cross-guideline comparison:**
This is where G2 (ACG 2019) differs most from G5 (AGA 2020). ACG makes strong recommendations for both vedolizumab and tofacitinib without preferring one over anti-TNF. AGA (2020) uses conditional recommendations and explicitly recommends infliximab or vedolizumab BEFORE tofacitinib in anti-TNF-naive patients, citing the JAK inhibitor's safety profile. G9 (ECCO 2022) similarly places tofacitinib after biologic failure. The ACG's equipoise reflects the timing -- these authors wrote the guideline as OCTAVE data were fresh and before the post-marketing safety signals (thrombosis, MACE) that led to FDA black-box warnings in 2021.

## Assessment

The "strong" ratings for vedolizumab and tofacitinib in anti-TNF-naive patients were aggressive for 2019 and would likely be softened today. The tofacitinib recommendation in particular has aged poorly: the FDA's 2021 safety warning (increased risk of MACE, thrombosis, malignancy with JAK inhibitors in the ORAL Surveillance trial in RA patients) has pushed tofacitinib later in treatment algorithms. This is not a flaw in the guideline -- it reflects the evidence available in 2019 -- but it underscores the rapid evolution of the advanced therapy landscape.
