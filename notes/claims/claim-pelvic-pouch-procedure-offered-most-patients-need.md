# steinhart-surg-3: The pelvic pouch (IPAA) is the standard restorative procedure for UC, with >90% patient satisfaction, but carries specific complications

**Source:** [[Steinhart 2018 - Ch8 Surgical Treatment]] — Steinhart — Crohn's and Colitis: Understanding and Managing IBD, 3E (2018), Ch 8

## The Claim

The pelvic pouch procedure is offered to most patients who need surgery for ulcerative colitis. More than 90% end up being very satisfied with the result.

## Evidence

Surgical case series and clinical experience. The pouchitis rate of 10-15% is cited, with the observation that "some centers that have a large experience with the pelvic pouch procedure report higher rates." Pouchitis is treated with antibiotics (ciprofloxacin) and responds differently from UC. Fertility impact is attributed to scarring around the fallopian tubes.

## Confidence

**Rating:** high
**Rationale:** HIGH for the general satisfaction rate and complication profile. The fertility impact is real and should be discussed with women of childbearing age considering IPAA -- this is a critical counseling p

## Stakes

IPAA revolutionized UC surgery by offering continence preservation. The trade-offs (bowel frequency, pouchitis risk, fertility impact) must be weighed against the alternative (permanent ileostomy) and against continued medical management. For young women planning pregnancy, the fertility concern may tip the decision toward delayed surgery or ileostomy.
