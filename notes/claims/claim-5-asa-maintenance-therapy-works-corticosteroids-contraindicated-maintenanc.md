# acg-uc2019-8: 5-ASA maintenance therapy works; corticosteroids are contraindicated for maintenance

**Source:** [[ACG UC 2019 - Rubin]] — Rubin et al. — ACG Clinical Guideline: Ulcerative Colitis (2019)

## The Claim

5-ASA maintenance therapy works; corticosteroids are contraindicated for maintenance

## Evidence

Meta-analysis of 11 trials: oral 5-ASA RR of relapse 0.65 (0.55-0.76) vs placebo. High-to-standard dose (≥2 g/d) superior to low dose (<2 g/d): RR relapse 0.79 (0.64-0.97). Topical 5-ASA RR relapse 0.60 (0.49-0.73) vs placebo. Corticosteroids are ineffective for maintenance and carry significant cumulative toxicity; the guideline emphasizes the shortest possible steroid course with early initiation of steroid-sparing therapy.

**Cross-guideline comparison:**
G5 and G9 both strongly recommend against steroids for maintenance and endorse 5-ASA maintenance. No divergence.

## Assessment

The steroid maintenance recommendation is appropriately strong -- this is settled medicine since the 1960s. The more nuanced question the guideline sidesteps: what to do with patients who repeatedly flare when 5-ASA is stopped? The "lifelong 5-ASA" paradigm has adherents and skeptics; the guideline suggests indefinite maintenance for most but doesn't define discontinuation criteria.
