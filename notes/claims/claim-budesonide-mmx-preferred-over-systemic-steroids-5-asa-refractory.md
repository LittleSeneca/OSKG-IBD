# acg-uc2019-7: Budesonide MMX is preferred over systemic steroids for 5-ASA-refractory mild-moderate UC; systemic steroids are reserved for failure or severe disease

**Source:** [[ACG UC 2019 - Rubin]] — Rubin et al. — ACG Clinical Guideline: Ulcerative Colitis (2019)

## The Claim

Budesonide MMX is preferred over systemic steroids for 5-ASA-refractory mild-moderate UC; systemic steroids are reserved for failure or severe disease

## Evidence

CORE I trial: budesonide MMX 9 mg achieved combined clinical + endoscopic remission in 17.9% vs 7.4% placebo (P=0.0143). In 5-ASA-refractory patients: budesonide MMX + 5-ASA superior to 5-ASA + placebo (P=0.049). Systemic corticosteroids: meta-analysis RR failure to remit 0.65 (0.45-0.93) vs placebo. Typical prednisone starting dose: 40-60 mg/d; no benefit beyond 60 mg/d. Response expected within 5-7 days.

**Cross-guideline comparison:**
G5 (AGA UC 2020) similarly positions budesonide MMX before systemic steroids. G9 (ECCO 2022) endorses the same sequencing but also discusses beclomethasone dipropionate (not available in the US) as an alternative topical steroid.

## Assessment

The sequencing of budesonide MMX before prednisone is a major step forward from the 2010 guideline. The high first-pass metabolism of budesonide means dramatically fewer steroid side effects -- a critical advantage for the predominantly young UC population. However, the absolute remission rate (17.9%) is modest; many patients will still need prednisone. The guideline appropriately separates mild-moderate (budesonide MMX OK) from severe (systemic steroids needed).
