# aga-cd2021-3: In biologic-naive patients, infliximab, adalimumab, or ustekinumab are preferred over certolizumab; in primary nonresponders to anti-TNF, ustekinumab is preferred (R2A-C)

**Source:** [[AGA Crohn's 2021 - Feuerstein]] — Feuerstein et al. — AGA Clinical Practice Guidelines: Crohn's Disease (2021)

## The Claim

In biologic-naive patients, infliximab, adalimumab, or ustekinumab are preferred over certolizumab; in primary nonresponders to anti-TNF, ustekinumab is preferred (R2A-C)

## Evidence

Among biologic-naive patients (8 RCTs, 1,458 patients): infliximab was superior to certolizumab pegol (OR 4.33, 95% CI 1.83-10.27) with moderate confidence. Infliximab trended toward superiority over vedolizumab (OR 2.20, 0.79-6.07) and ustekinumab (OR 2.14, 0.89-5.15) but confidence intervals crossed unity. Ustekinumab (OR 2.02, 1.09-3.75) and adalimumab (OR 2.97, 1.16-6.70) were superior to certolizumab pegol. No significant differences among adalimumab, ustekinumab, and vedolizumab as first-line agents (very low certainty).

Among anti-TNF-exposed patients (6 RCTs, 1,606 patients): ustekinumab was superior to placebo (OR 2.58, 1.50-4.44) with moderate certainty. Adalimumab in patients with prior infliximab intolerance or secondary nonresponse was effective (OR 3.57, 1.66-7.65). Vedolizumab's effect was uncertain (OR 1.53, 0.77-3.06) with wide confidence intervals crossing unity — hence the conditional recommendation.

## Assessment

The certolizumab downgrade is the sharpest edge in this guideline. It's based on indirect evidence (no head-to-head trials), and certolizumab's unique attributes (subcutaneous only, no placental transfer in third trimester — making it preferred in pregnancy) are not captured in the network meta-analysis. The guideline's treatment of secondary nonresponse is pragmatic but imprecise: "secondary nonresponse to infliximab" encompasses both immune-mediated failure (anti-drug antibodies) and non-immune pharmacokinetic failure (rapid clearance from high inflammatory burden). TDM-guided decision-making (the 2017 AGA guideline) would direct the former to same-class switch and the latter to dose escalation — but this guideline's R2C recommends adalimumab or ustekinumab regardless of mechanism. The tension between these two AGA guidelines (2021 biologic selection vs. 2017 TDM) is unresolved.
