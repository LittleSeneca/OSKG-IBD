# chutkan-dysbiosis-1: Dysbiosis is a distinct clinical entity, primarily caused by antibiotic overuse, that underlies many of the vague GI and systemic symptoms seen in modern gastroenterology practice

**Source:** [[The Microbiome Solution - Chutkan - Ch5 Dysbiosis]] — Chutkan — The Microbiome Solution (2015), Ch 5

## The Claim

Dysbiosis is an alteration of the microbial community that diminishes a person's essential population of good bacteria and allows pathogenic (bad) bacteria that are normally present in low amounts to flourish — in essence, it is a microbial imbalance in or on your body.

## Evidence

Clinical observation from Chutkan's Georgetown gastroenterology practice. She describes the shift from seeing well-defined diseases (ulcers, colitis, cancer) to seeing symptom complexes without visible pathology. She notes that standard diagnostic tools (endoscopy, colonoscopy, capsule endoscopy, imaging, blood work) return normal results in these patients despite significant symptoms.
