# thompson-een-4: Elemental diet duration should be guided by disease severity: 6 weeks for severe disease (bleeding, fistulas, surgery risk), 3-4 weeks for moderate symptoms, 2 weeks for food allergy testing

**Source:** [[Thompson 2013 - Elemental Diet Protocol]] — Thompson — The IBD Remission Diet (2013)

## The Claim

Duration guidelines: moderate-to-severe bleeding or fistulas = 6+ weeks (up to 3 months for fistulas); sporadic bleeding = 4-6 weeks; minor symptoms (bloating, gas, pain, diarrhea) = 3-4 weeks; food allergy testing = 10-14 days. "Stay on it for at least one to two more weeks after ALL symptoms have cleared." Thompson's gastroenterologist advised: 3 or 6 weeks as standard; if 3 weeks fails, full 6 weeks required (9 weeks total).

## Evidence

Clinical experience and Thompson's gastroenterologist's protocol. No controlled data on optimal EEN duration. The 6-week standard maps to corticosteroid tapering timelines in IBD.

## Stakes

Longer elemental diet = higher cost, greater compliance burden, more social disruption. If 3 weeks works as well as 6 for moderate disease, the shorter protocol would dramatically improve accessibility.

## Assessment

The duration guidelines are reasonable clinical defaults but entirely unvalidated. Thompson's emphasis on intuition ("ask your body") alongside clinical guidelines is both wise (acknowledging individual variation) and problematic (substituting feelings for evidence).
