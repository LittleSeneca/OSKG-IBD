# pimentel-ch9-1: Peppermint oil is one of the best-validated natural treatments for IBS -- it works as a calcium channel blocker and weak 5-HT3 antagonist

**Source:** [[Pimentel 2022 - Ch9 Refractory SIBO]] — Pimentel & Rezaie — The Microbiome Connection (2022), Ch 9

## The Claim

Peppermint appears to work in multiple ways. It is a calcium channel blocker... It blocks calcium channels in the smooth muscle from the esophagus all the way to the rectum and relaxes muscles as it travels.

## Evidence

One robust RCT for IBS diarrhea. Mechanism: calcium channel blockade relaxes smooth muscle (anti-spasmodic), weak 5-HT3 antagonism slows transit. Enteric-coated formulations release in the small intestine. Peppermint oil can also relieve esophageal spasm (4 drops in water).

## Assessment

Peppermint oil is one of the few natural treatments Pimentel endorses with enthusiasm. Its multi-mechanism action (smooth muscle relaxation, transit slowing, possible antibacterial effects) makes it broadly applicable. It is safe, inexpensive, and has better evidence than most probiotics. For patients with residual cramping or diarrhea after SIBO treatment, it is a reasonable first-line add-on.
