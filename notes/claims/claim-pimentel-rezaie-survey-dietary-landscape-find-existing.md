# pimentel-lfe-1: Existing dietary approaches (lactose avoidance, gluten-free, low-FODMAP, SCD, Paleo) are either insufficiently specific to SIBO or unnecessarily restrictive

**Source:** [[Pimentel 2022 - Low-Fermentation Eating]] — Pimentel & Rezaie — The Microbiome Connection (2022), LFE

## The Claim

Pimentel and Rezaie survey the dietary landscape and find all existing approaches wanting. Lactose avoidance: "patients who have lingering gastrointestinal issues may well have an enzyme deficiency that leads to lactose intolerance, but their continuing symptoms are likely due to IBS/SIBO." Gluten-free: "if you have SIBO and eat wheat, you will have symptoms... When we remedy their SIBO, their intolerance to gluten and wheat goes away." Low-FODMAP: "the low-FODMAP diet is not easy to follow... 207 food ingredients to avoid... it's difficult to go on the low-FODMAP diet and lead a normal life... your microbiome may shift in a bad direction with the low-FODMAP diet." SCD: "one clinical study compared the low-FODMAP diet to the Specific Carbohydrate Diet for IBS patients, and it was found that the low-FODMAP diet relieved symptoms but the Specific Carbohydrate Diet did not."

## Evidence

Clinical experience at Cedars-Sinai (thousands of patients). One referenced clinical trial comparing low-FODMAP to SCD for IBS (finding low-FODMAP superior). Breath test data showing lactose intolerance resolves in 30-40% of SIBO patients after successful SIBO treatment. The FODMAP content of wheat as alternative explanation for gluten sensitivity.

## Confidence

**Rating:** high
**Rationale:** MEDIUM-HIGH for the clinical observations (thousands of patients at the leading SIBO center). LOW for the comparative claim that SCD doesn't work for IBS -- one trial, and SCD was designed for IBD, no

## Stakes

If Pimentel is right that most "food sensitivities" in SIBO/IBS are actually SIBO-driven fermentation reactions rather than true allergies or intolerances, then treating SIBO should resolve these sensitivities and patients should not need permanent restrictive diets. This is a radically different model from SCD or food allergy approaches that assume permanent intolerance.

## Disagreement

**Who disagrees:** Celiac specialists would strongly object to the implication that gluten sensitivity in SIBO patients means gluten isn't the problem. Celiac disease is a distinct autoimmune condition requiring permanent gluten avoidance regardless of SIBO status. Gottschall would object to the claim that SCD doesn't work -- her evidence base is clinical observation, same as Pimentel's.

**Alternative reading:** Not documented.

## Assessment

Pimentel's dietary survey is the most authoritative critique of existing SIBO diets from the leading SIBO researcher. His dismissal of SCD and gluten-free as unnecessary for SIBO is evidence-informed but somewhat dismissive of patient experience. The key insight -- that successful SIBO treatment often resolves food intolerances -- is supported by his lactose intolerance reversal data and is a genuinely important clinical finding.
