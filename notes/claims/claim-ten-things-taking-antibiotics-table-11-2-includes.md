# chutkan-rewild-2: When antibiotics are unavoidable, a protocol of probiotics, prebiotic foods, sugar elimination, and liver support can mitigate microbiome damage

**Source:** [[The Microbiome Solution - Chutkan - Ch11 Rewilding Illness]] — Chutkan — The Microbiome Solution (2015), Ch 11

## The Claim

The "Ten Things to Do When Taking Antibiotics" (Table 11-2) includes: (1) take a probiotic during and for at least one month after antibiotics, staggered from antibiotic doses; (2) request narrow-spectrum rather than broad-spectrum antibiotics when culture results permit; (3) eat prebiotic foods high in fiber and resistant starch; (4) eliminate sugary/starchy foods that feed yeast overgrowth; (5) eat yeast-fighting foods (onion, garlic, coconut oil); (6) drink ginger tea for GI comfort; (7) use bentonite clay for antibiotic-associated diarrhea; (8) drink mushroom tea (shiitake, maitake) for immune support; (9) support liver function with leafy greens and cruciferous vegetables; (10) stop acid-suppressing drugs 72 hours before antibiotics to restore stomach acid barrier function.

## Evidence

For probiotics: cited studies showing reduction in antibiotic-associated diarrhea and C. difficile risk. For narrow-spectrum antibiotics: culture-directed therapy is standard infectious disease practice. For dietary recommendations: mechanistic reasoning based on prebiotic/anti-yeast principles.
