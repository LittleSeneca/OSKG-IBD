# pimentel-evol-7: Despite 16 years of mechanism discovery, SIBO treatment efficacy improved only modestly: rifaximin's 44% response rate in TARGET 3 (2015) is not drama

**Source:** [[Pimentel 2006 - SIBO Theory Evolution 2006-2022]] — Pimentel — A New IBS Solution (2006) vs The Microbiome Connection (2022)

## The Claim

Despite 16 years of mechanism discovery, SIBO treatment efficacy improved only modestly: rifaximin's 44% response rate in TARGET 3 (2015) is not dramatically better than neomycin's 35% response in the 2003 study. The real advance is in treatment specificity (gas-type matching) and prevention (prokinetic optimization), not in treatment efficacy.

## Evidence

2003 double-blind study: neomycin 35% symptom improvement vs 11% placebo. Among those whose breath tests normalized, 75% had IBS improvement. 2015 TARGET 3 trial: rifaximin 44% response rate for IBS-D. The absolute improvement from 35% to 44% over 12 years is modest. The guideline notes that the real advances are in gas-type-specific protocol selection and prokinetic optimization for relapse prevention, not in the magnitude of antibiotic response.

## Stakes

This is the most sobering finding in the evolutionary analysis. It suggests that the ceiling of antibiotic monotherapy for SIBO may be ~40-50%, and that further mechanistic understanding alone will not improve outcomes — what is needed is better therapeutic agents or combination approaches.

## Assessment

The weakest evolutionary advance and the most honest observation. The 2006-2022 journey is a story of vastly improved understanding with only modestly improved treatment. This pattern (mechanism discovery outpacing therapeutic advance) is common in translational medicine but rarely stated so plainly.
