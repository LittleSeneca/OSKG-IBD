# chutkan-rewild-4: Antibiotic use for acne is a major risk factor for later IBD, and dermatology's routine use of long-term oral antibiotics represents a significant public health problem

**Source:** [[The Microbiome Solution - Chutkan - Ch11 Rewilding Illness]] — Chutkan — The Microbiome Solution (2015), Ch 11

## The Claim

Almost all of the patients I see in my practice with Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis have a history of taking antibiotics for acne, and while that doesn't necessarily prove causation in those particular patients, the medical literature is full of studies that now confirm that antibiotic use, particularly in childhood, is a major risk factor for the development of these diseases.

## Evidence

Cites the epidemiological literature. Describes acne antibiotics as especially damaging because: they are broad-spectrum, taken for months or years, and highly active against gut bacteria. Notes that dermatologists often tell teenagers "what they eat doesn't have any effect on their skin" while prescribing years of microbiome-damaging antibiotics — a practice Chutkan finds indefensible.
