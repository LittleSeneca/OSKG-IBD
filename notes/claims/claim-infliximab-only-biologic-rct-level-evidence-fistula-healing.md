# ecco-cd2020-10: Infliximab is the only biologic with RCT-level evidence for fistula healing; adalimumab has weaker evidence; ustekinumab and vedolizumab lack sufficient evidence (R3.1-R3.5)

**Source:** [[ECCO Crohn's Medical 2020 - Torres]] — Torres et al. — ECCO Guidelines on Crohn's Disease: Medical Management (2020)

## The Claim

Infliximab is the only biologic with RCT-level evidence for fistula healing; adalimumab has weaker evidence; ustekinumab and vedolizumab lack sufficient evidence (R3.1-R3.5)

## Evidence

This is the clearest evidence hierarchy in the entire guideline:

**Infliximab (Gold standard):** Two dedicated RCTs. Induction: 46% vs. 12.9% complete fistula closure (RR 3.57; 95% CI 1.38-9.25). Maintenance (ACCENT II): 34.4% vs. 19.2% at Week 54 (RR 1.79; 95% CI 1.10-2.92). Higher infliximab trough levels associated with better fistula healing in observational data.

**Adalimumab (Secondary endpoint):** CHARM post-hoc analysis (n=117 with fistulae at baseline): superiority over placebo for fistula healing at 56 weeks (RR 2.57; 95% CI 1.13-5.84). CHOICE open-label trial: 39% complete fistula healing after infliximab failure. Evidence was downgraded because fistula healing was a secondary endpoint in a post-hoc analysis.

**Ustekinumab (Post-hoc only):** CERTIFI/UNITI post-hoc (n=238): trend favoring ustekinumab for fistula response but NOT statistically significant for induction (RR 1.77; 95% CI 0.93-3.37) or maintenance.

**Vedolizumab (Post-hoc only):** GEMINI 2 post-hoc (n=45 with complex perianal fistulae): trend favoring vedolizumab (RR 2.23; 95% CI 0.57-8.72), not statistically significant. Dedicated RCT (NCT02630966) prematurely stopped for slow recruitment.

**Cross-guideline comparison:**
G1 (ACG 2018, R35-R37) gives strong recommendations for infliximab (moderate evidence), adalimumab (low evidence), and certolizumab pegol (low evidence) for perianal fistulas. G8 is MORE conservative on adalimumab (weak, very low evidence) and does not endorse certolizumab for fistulas at all (not approved in EU). This is a significant divergence: ECCO's evidence standards for fistula healing are stricter, reflecting the European regulatory environment where adalimumab is not formally indicated for fistulizing CD.

## Assessment

G8's fistula recommendations are more conservative than G1's — and likely more honest. The evidence hierarchy (infliximab >>> adalimumab >>> ustekinumab/vedolizumab) is well-supported. Clinicians using adalimumab for fistulas need to understand they're operating on post-hoc secondary endpoint data. The "insufficient evidence" for ustekinumab and vedolizumab in fistulas is an important restraint on off-label use, though the guideline acknowledges these agents may be considered when anti-TNFs are ineffective or contraindicated (narrative text, not a formal recommendation).
