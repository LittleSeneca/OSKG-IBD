## Assessment

The inconsistency across studies is real and remains a problem in 2024. The key methodological insight -- that single-point faecal sampling in a condition with unstable microbiota and fluctuating symptoms is inherently limited -- is important and underappreciated in the clinical literature. The studies that cluster IBS patients into distinct microbiota profiles (Rajilic-Stojanovic, Jeffery) suggest that the heterogeneity is not just noise -- there may be genuine IBS subgroups with different microbiota signatures -- but larger longitudinal studies with symptom-linked sampling are needed.
