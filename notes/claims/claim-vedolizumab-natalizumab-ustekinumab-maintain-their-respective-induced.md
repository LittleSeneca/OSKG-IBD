# acg-cd2018-24: Vedolizumab, natalizumab, and ustekinumab maintain their respective induced remissions (R51-R53)

**Source:** [[ACG Crohn's 2018 - Lichtenstein]] — Lichtenstein et al. — ACG Clinical Guideline: Management of Crohn's Disease (2018)

## The Claim

Vedolizumab, natalizumab, and ustekinumab maintain their respective induced remissions (R51-R53)

## Evidence

All three agents have positive maintenance RCT data (GEMINI 2 for vedolizumab, ENCORE/ENABLE for natalizumab, UNITI-IM for ustekinumab). Natalizumab's PML restriction is re-emphasized. Vedolizumab may be used as monotherapy but combination with an immunomodulator may be considered to reduce immunogenicity. Ustekinumab maintenance is subcutaneous (90 mg q8w).

## Assessment

These conditional recommendations (vs. strong for anti-TNF maintenance) reflect the smaller evidence base and, in natalizumab's case, the safety restriction. The guideline doesn't address the practical question: should patients who achieve remission on one biologic be maintained indefinitely, or is there a role for treatment de-escalation? This is addressed in later guidelines.
