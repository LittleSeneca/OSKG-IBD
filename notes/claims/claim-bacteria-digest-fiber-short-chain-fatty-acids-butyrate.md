# chutkan-found-3: Gut bacteria perform essential metabolic, immune, and neurological functions that the human body cannot perform alone

**Source:** [[The Microbiome Solution - Chutkan - Intro Ch1-3]] — Chutkan — The Microbiome Solution (2015), Intro + Ch 1-3

## The Claim

Bacteria digest fiber into short-chain fatty acids (butyrate, propionate, acetate) that feed colonocytes, regulate immune function, and signal the brain. They synthesize vitamins (K, B12, biotin), metabolize drugs, detoxify carcinogens, and train the immune system to distinguish self from non-self. They influence mood, appetite, and behavior through the gut-brain axis.
