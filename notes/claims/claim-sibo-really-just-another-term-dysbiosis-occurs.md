# chutkan-dysbiosis-3: SIBO is a form of dysbiosis that should be diagnosed clinically, with breath testing as an adjunct rather than a gold standard

**Source:** [[The Microbiome Solution - Chutkan - Ch5 Dysbiosis]] — Chutkan — The Microbiome Solution (2015), Ch 5

## The Claim

SIBO is really just another term for dysbiosis that occurs when large amounts of not-so-good bacteria take up residence in the small intestine.

## Evidence

Chutkan presents a case study of a dermatologist's husband who developed SIBO after unnecessary antibiotics for Salmonella, in the setting of chronic PPI use. The resolution required stopping the PPI, adding probiotics, and dietary changes — not more antibiotics.
