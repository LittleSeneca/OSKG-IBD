# yamada-cd64-5: Vedolizumab and ustekinumab provide mechanistically distinct alternatives to anti-TNF with favorable safety profiles

**Source:** [[Yamada 7E - Ch64 Crohn's Disease Management]] — Yamada's Textbook of Gastroenterology, 7E (2022), Ch 64

## The Claim

Vedolizumab "is an effective induction and maintenance agent approved for use in CD," with "up to 89% of patients in remission after 3 years" among initial responders, though "the efficacy of vedolizumab is lower among those with prior anti-TNF failure." Ustekinumab "is an effective induction and maintenance agent for CD among patients who are experienced or naive to anti-TNF therapy." Neither carries the boxed warnings for serious infections or lymphoma that anti-TNF agents carry.

## Evidence

Vedolizumab: pivotal trials (GEMINI), post-hoc analyses showing trough level-response relationships, immunogenicity of ~5%. Ustekinumab: UNITI trials, immunogenicity <5% through week 156, higher trough levels correlated with clinical remission. The authors note that drug level monitoring for these agents is less established than for anti-TNF.

## Confidence

**Rating:** high
**Rationale:** HIGH for efficacy in both anti-TNF-naive and experienced populations (ustekinumab stronger in experienced). HIGH for safety advantage. MEDIUM for the clinical utility of TDM with vedolizumab/ustekinum

## Stakes

These drugs have expanded second-line options dramatically. For elderly patients, those with infection risk, or those with cancer history, vedolizumab or ustekinumab may be preferred over anti-TNF as first-line biologic (see Table 64.4 in the chapter). The absence of boxed warnings is clinically meaningful.

## Assessment

The authors present a balanced comparison. The vedolizumab section appropriately notes its lower efficacy in anti-TNF-experienced patients. The ustekinumab section highlights its lower immunogenicity. The Table 64.4 (special considerations) is clinically useful: abscess → drainage + antibiotics/biologics; TB+ → vedolizumab/thiopurine/methotrexate; melanoma history → vedolizumab; older age → vedolizumab or ustekinumab.
