# pimentel-lfe-4: Meal spacing is as important as food choice -- constant eating impairs the migrating motor complex

**Source:** [[Pimentel 2022 - Low-Fermentation Eating]] — Pimentel & Rezaie — The Microbiome Connection (2022), LFE

## The Claim

We can't emphasize enough the importance of keeping the gastrointestinal tract's cleaning waves intact on a regular basis. These cleaning waves... don't occur until you are fasting... We recommend waiting four to five hours between each meal... Snacking between meals is a no-no. If you eat every two hours, you won't allow the small intestine to clear properly... Just one bite of food can impair your cleaning waves!

## Evidence

Pimentel's MMC research (published in multiple papers). The physiological mechanism: MMC phase III (cleaning waves) occurs only during fasting state; feeding suppresses MMC activity; bacterial clearance depends on MMC function; SIBO patients have reduced MMC activity (possibly due to anti-vinculin antibodies from prior gastroenteritis).

## Confidence

**Rating:** high
**Rationale:** HIGH. The MMC mechanism is Pimentel's signature scientific contribution and is well-supported by his published research. The 4-5 hour interval is based on normal gastric emptying time (1-4 hours) plus

## Stakes

Meal spacing is a zero-cost, zero-side-effect intervention that should be part of EVERY SIBO protocol regardless of which specific diet is chosen. It's the convergence point where Pimentel's motility theory meets dietary practice.

## Disagreement

**Who disagrees:** No serious disagreement on the MMC mechanism. The debate is whether meal spacing alone (without dietary change or prokinetics) is sufficient for SIBO management. Pimentel uses it alongside the LFE diet and often prokinetics.

## Assessment

This is the single most evidence-based dietary recommendation in the entire SIBO/IBD dietary literature. Meal spacing should be standard of care. The 4-5 hour interval is a reasonable default based on gastric emptying physiology; individualized timing based on gastric emptying studies would be ideal but impractical.
