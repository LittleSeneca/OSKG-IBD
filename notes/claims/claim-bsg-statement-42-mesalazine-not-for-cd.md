# bsg-ibd2019-7: Statement 42 (strong, moderate): Mesalazine should NOT be used for induction or maintenance of remission in Crohn's disease. This strong negative reco

**Source:** [[BSG IBD 2019 - Lamb]] — Lamb et al. — BSG Consensus Guidelines on IBD (2019), Gut 68(Suppl 3):s1-s106

## The Claim

Statement 42 (strong, moderate): Mesalazine should NOT be used for induction or maintenance of remission in Crohn's disease. This strong negative recommendation is consistent with ACG 2018.

## Evidence

97.7% agreement. The guideline cites multiple RCTs and meta-analyses showing no benefit of mesalazine over placebo for CD induction or maintenance. This is one of the strongest negative recommendations in the guideline, reflecting the consistency and quality of the evidence against mesalazine in CD.

## Confidence

**Rating:** very-high

## Stakes

Despite this strong recommendation and consistent evidence, mesalazine continues to be prescribed for CD in clinical practice — particularly by non-specialists. This recommendation directly addresses a common practice gap.
