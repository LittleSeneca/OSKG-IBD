**Source:** [[Inflamed - Marya Patel 2021]] — Marya & Patel — Inflamed: Deep Medicine and the Anatomy of Injustice (2021)

## The Claim

Inflammation is the body's response to damage. The damage is not just bacterial, viral, or dietary — it is also social, economic, and environmental. Colonialism, racism, poverty, pollution, industrial food systems, and the erosion of community are inflammatory forces that literally change human biology: they alter the microbiome, dysregulate the immune system, accelerate cellular aging, and produce the chronic diseases that dominate modern medicine. The "anatomy of injustice" is written in inflamed bodies. Treating these bodies with pharmaceuticals without addressing the social determinants of inflammation is medically incomplete — it manages symptoms while leaving the disease-producing structures intact. Deep medicine requires both individual healing and collective action to change the conditions that produce disease.
