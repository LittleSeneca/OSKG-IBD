# SIBO-Diet-Comparison: Head-to-Head Dietary Therapy Evidence for SIBO

**Question:** Which dietary protocol (SCD, low-FODMAP, LFE) is best supported by evidence for treating SIBO?

**Source:** Phase 4 evidence brief [[EB-Diet-Comparison]]

**Status:** Open. No head-to-head trial exists.

**Research Gap Description:**
Three major dietary protocols target SIBO through carbohydrate restriction (SCD, low-FODMAP, LFE), all operating through the same mechanism (reducing fermentable substrate). Low-FODMAP has the best formal RCT evidence but in IBS, not SIBO. SCD has the strongest mechanistic narrative and clinical tradition. LFE has the simplest implementation but no published trials. No study has compared any two of these diets for SIBO-specific outcomes.

**What Would Resolve It:**
1. A randomized trial of SCD vs. low-FODMAP for SIBO-specific endpoints: breath test normalization rate at 4, 8, and 12 weeks
2. A three-arm trial adding LFE as a comparator
3. A study testing whether LFE's meal-spacing rule (4-5 hours between meals) adds benefit beyond carbohydrate restriction alone

**Why It Matters:**
Dietary therapy is the only modality for SIBO that patients can self-administer indefinitely, without prescription cost, and without antibiotic resistance risk. If the three diets are indeed mechanistically equivalent (all reduce fermentable substrate), the choice should be driven by compliance and patient preference. If one is meaningfully more effective, it should be the first-line recommendation.
