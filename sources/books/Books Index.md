## Notes

- Sleisenger and Yamada are massive (2,500+ pages each, multi-volume). Only IBD-relevant chapters need extraction.
- Pimentel's two books (A New IBS Solution and The Microbiome Connection) represent the evolution of SIBO theory from 2006 to 2022. Both are essential for understanding the diagnostic debate.
- Gottschall's Breaking the Vicious Cycle (1994) is the foundational text for the Specific Carbohydrate Diet, one of the most studied dietary interventions for IBD.
