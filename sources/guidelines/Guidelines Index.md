## Notes

- All 11 guidelines acquired on 2026-08-01. PDFs in _pdfs/ (gitignored). Text files in this directory.
- G1 has both a summary version (248KB, 34K chars) from ACG CDN and the full journal version (479KB, 260K chars) from Sci-Hub.
- G4 (AGA SIBO) and G5 (AGA UC) acquired via Sci-Hub CDN (sci.bban.top). G11 (BSG) also via Sci-Hub.
- ECCO guidelines (G7-G9) acquired from institutional repositories (darmzentrum-bern.ch, iris.uniroma1.it).
- G10 (Rome Foundation) is the "Intestinal Microbiota in Functional Bowel Disorders: A Rome Foundation Report" which covers SIBO within functional GI disorders.
- Guidelines are regulatory/clinical bedrock but evolve slowly. The 2018-2022 window is current.
- The SIBO guidelines (G3, G4, G10) are particularly important because they document the debate over breath testing methodology and diagnostic thresholds.
