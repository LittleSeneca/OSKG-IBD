# Book Guide — OSKG-IBD

The canonical reading list for the IBD/SIBO knowledge graph. Books are organized into four tiers by their role in the graph, not by general reading value.

## Tier Definitions

| Tier | Label | Treatment |
|------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Foundational | Full chapter-by-chapter extraction. The graph cannot function without these. |
| 2 | Specialized | Targeted claims extraction. Fills specific gaps Tier 1 doesn't cover. |
| 3 | Clinical Guidelines | Regulatory and clinical bedrock. Treated as primary sources. |
| 4 | Adjacent | Cross-reference only. Useful frameworks outside the core IBD/SIBO domain. |

## Key People

Influential researchers and clinicians without standalone books (or whose contribution is primarily in papers):

- **Dr. Ali Rezaie** — Co-author of The Microbiome Connection. Cedars-Sinai. Key papers on breath testing and SIBO diagnosis.
- **Dr. Allison Siebecker, ND** — Leading SIBO clinician-educator. Creator of the SIBO Specific Food Guide. Runs siboinfo.com. No book, but the SIBO Pro Course is the most comprehensive clinical education resource.
- **Dr. Nirala Jacobi, ND** — The SIBO Doctor. Extensive podcast and educational content on SIBO treatment protocols.
- **Dr. David Suskind** — Seattle Children's. Key papers on SCD for pediatric IBD.
- **Dr. James Lewis** — UPenn. Key papers on diet, microbiome, and IBD (DINE-CD study).
- **Dr. R. Balfour Sartor** — UNC. Leading IBD microbiome researcher.

## Reading Order

1. **Pimentel (2006), then Pimentel & Rezaie (2022)** — The SIBO thesis and its 16-year evolution. Start here because SIBO is the graph's organizing controversy.
2. **Steinhart (2018)** — The patient-facing IBD overview. Accessible before the heavy textbooks.
3. **Sleisenger/Fordtran IBD chapters** — The clinical canon. Use the Steinhart framing to navigate.
4. **Yamada IBD chapters** — Compare against Sleisenger for points of divergence.
5. **Tier 2 SIBO books (Sarna, LaPine)** — Patient protocols and recipes. Targeted extraction.
6. **Tier 2 Microbiome (Enders, Mayer, Bulsiewicz, Yong)** — Builds the conceptual infrastructure.
7. **Tier 2 Diet (Gottschall, Foote, Thompson)** — Dietary intervention canon.
8. **Tier 3 Guidelines** — Concurrent with diet books. Compare guideline recommendations against patient protocols.
9. **Tier 4 Adjacent** — Last. Cross-reference only.
